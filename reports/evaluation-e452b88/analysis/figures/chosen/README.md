## Chosen 04: Zero-Shot vs Few-Shot Metric Bars

Models on x, native metric scores on y; zero-shot (blue) beside few-shot (orange). Three general-metric figures and six terminology figures cover all three datasets and both annotation views. All/external/LLM-enriched terminology groups have separate panels. These are identical copies of the prompting bar figures, not new scores.

Axes include zero. Unavailable scores are NA, not zero; universally unavailable success-rate metrics are omitted. Shared-direction means do not establish matched rows or statistical significance.
