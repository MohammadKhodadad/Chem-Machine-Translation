# Combined Language Correlations

Each model has one combined native score for From-language performance and one for To-language performance. From German, for example, averages German -> all supported targets; From French averages French -> all supported targets. Scatter plots compare those combined model means, with German/French first.

First average supported partner directions equally within each model/dataset/prompt. Then average the dataset/prompt means equally. Every dataset and both prompts are required; incomplete languages are marked in the model-score CSV and omitted from correlations, never filled with zero. Native metrics and terminology groups remain separate. Heatmaps show to-to, from-from and from-to correlations.

Partner sets can differ between languages and datasets. These are averages over supported partners, not every possible language or matched partner populations. Cross-role means can share directions. Overall model strength and partner composition may drive correlations; no independence, significance or causal claims are made.
