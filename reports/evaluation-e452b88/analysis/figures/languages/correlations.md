# Language Correlations: To and From

Three panels per native metric compare To vs To, From vs From, and From vs To. Cross-role panels have source languages on rows and target languages on columns. Pearson and Spearman are shown separately, with datasets, prompts, metrics and terminology groups kept separate.

Each observation is one model. For that model, To-language scores average all supported incoming directions equally; From-language scores average outgoing directions equally. Models are never pooled as translation-level samples. Scores are native means, not within-model rank percentiles. All retained models and both prompts must have scored direction support; missing data is not zero.

Each language has identical partner support across models, but different languages can have different partner sets. Partners are NOT matched across languages; correlations may reflect partner composition and overall model strength. Cross-role means can share directions: English -> French contributes to both From English and To French. These are not independent ability measurements. No statistical significance, independence or causal claims are made. Constant vectors or fewer than three models give NA.
